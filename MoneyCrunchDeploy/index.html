Common questions

What you should know before continuing.

Straight answers, without exaggerated claims or pressure.

Does this pre-check affect my credit score?

No. MoneyCrunch does not request a credit report or perform a credit check. A provider may explain its own verification process if you continue.

Does MoneyCrunch collect my personal information?

No contact details are collected in this pre-check. Your selections are processed in your browser. If you continue, the provider's privacy policy applies.

Is MoneyCrunch the debt-relief provider?

No. MoneyCrunch is an independent education and referral site. CuraDebt reviews inquiries and may connect eligible consumers with independent providers.

How does MoneyCrunch earn money?

We may receive compensation when someone completes a qualifying action through a partner link. This does not increase the visitor's cost or change the pre-check result.
